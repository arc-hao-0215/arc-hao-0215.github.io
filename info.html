Buildings, landscapes,

Hao Chang is an architectural designer and an incoming M.Arch II student at Yale School of Architecture (Fall 2027). A graduate of Tsinghua University, his work moves between architectural design, urban infrastructure, and environmental research.

As a 2025 SOM Foundation China Fellow, he has pursued research on flood resilience and community infrastructure in Jakarta. His experience spans professional practice at Kohn Pedersen Fox and JJP Architects & Planners, cultural engagement at the Hong Kong Palace Museum, and commissioned architectural work at Tsinghua University.

Photography, drawing, performance, and editorial work offer other ways of observing and engaging with space.
