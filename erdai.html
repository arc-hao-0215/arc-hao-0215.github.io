Erdai Art Museum

Penghu, TaiwanArchitecture & adaptive reuse2024

How can a landscape of defence become a place of reflection?

A former coastal battery becomes a route through art, ruin, and sea. The museum follows the cliff’s contours and slips beneath the ground, allowing inherited military structures and new cultural spaces to share the same landscape.

01

An inherited ground.

Bunkers, trenches, and artillery positions become the starting points of a cultural route. Existing structures are retained as spaces for arrival, display, and observation.
