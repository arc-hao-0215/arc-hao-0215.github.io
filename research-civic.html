Knowledge as civic infrastructure.

Abstract

A library can be read as a collection of rooms, but also as an arrangement of relationships between knowledge and public space. Between Chang’an Avenue and the hutongs, this enquiry tests whether a civic building can connect urban movement, shared learning, and environmental systems through its section.

Research question

What can the section of a library reveal about the relationship between public access, structure, and collective life?

Method

Sectional perspectives, programme studies, and physical models investigate suspended volumes and a permeable ground. The same section is read through spatial, structural, climatic, and programmatic relationships. This method makes it possible to consider the systems together while examining what each contributes to public occupation.

Public space beyond the ground floor

The open ground establishes a connection with pedestrian movement. Above it, gardens, bridges, reading spaces, and shared rooms extend the idea of a public interior vertically. The enquiry concerns the continuity between these spaces: whether moving through the building can also become an encounter with learning and other people.

Structure as a civic decision

Suspended volumes and a large spanning structure are not only questions of engineering expression. They determine what can remain open beneath the building and how programmes relate across it. Physical models test the spatial proposition alongside the sectional drawings. Structure becomes one of the means by which a civic room is made available to the city.

Reading environmental and social systems together

Solar roof systems, ventilation, rainwater capture, and interior gardens are considered within the same architectural section as reading and gathering spaces. The resulting drawings propose relationships rather than measured outcomes. The research contribution lies in the method of reading the building as an interconnected civic and environmental system; operational performance would need separate verification.
