Books Above Bustles

Urban Library Design

Xidan, BeijingKnowledge · Civic Life · InfrastructureAcademic Studio
Project proposition

A library that is also a route through the city.

At Xidan, the monumental scale of West Chang’an Avenue meets a finer-grained neighbourhood and a busy subway interchange. A civic building at this threshold could do more than occupy a closed block: it could offer the city a new public ground.

Books Above Bustles imagines a layered library where a lifted institutional volume frames a landscape of shared rooms. Reading, learning, workspaces, gardens and gathering spaces connect through a single spatial sequence.

01 / Context & urban issue

Between two scales of the city.

West Chang’an Avenue meets Xidan’s smaller neighbourhood streets and a busy subway interchange. The site raises a civic question: how might a library reconnect pedestrian routes and create public ground rather than reinforce an enclosed block?

02 / Design approach

Four operations, one public continuum.

Lift the library to open the ground, carve a central atrium, connect programmes through bridges and terraces, and investigate an outer screen and roof as environmental boundaries. These operations build one continuous civic sequence.

01 / Lift

Floating & Shifting

Suspended volumes clear the ground for public landscape and cross-avenue views.

02 / Open

Opening

A central atrium cuts through the volume, bringing daylight towards civic spaces below.

03 / Connect

Linking

Bridges and terraces connect offices, reading spaces and community programmes.

04 / Moderate

Passive Strategies

The screened envelope and roof garden propose ways to support daylight, ventilation and comfort.

03 / Architecture

A vertical public realm.

The project is best understood in section: a shared urban room beneath the raised volume, layered civic programmes, and a central void linking different levels and degrees of public access.

A / Section

Multiple grounds within one building.

The sectional perspective connects the subway threshold, public rooms, education, reading, workspaces and roof garden across a single vertical space.

B / Plans

Programmes arranged around a shared void.

The plans clarify how the changing programmes retain their own character while staying connected to the atrium and circulation network.

C / Movement

Circulation becomes an encounter.

The exploded axonometric traces connected routes and terraces through learning, gathering, reading and workspaces.

01 / Arrive The route begins at the subway threshold and opens into a shared urban living room.

02 / Connect Bridges and terraces link programmes around the central void.

03 / Ascend Upper reading rooms and roof gardens extend the civic route vertically.

04 / Civic experience

More than a place to read.

Public arrival, stepped rooms, bookshelves and elevated walkways allow everyday reading to coexist with gathering, events and the activity of the surrounding city.

A / Shared interior

From the subway to the public living room.

Stepped platforms bring movement and informal gathering into the library; the central hall extends the same civic atmosphere through its upper levels.

B / Reading and city

A common interior with a civic presence.

Reading terraces overlook collective activities, while the aerial perspective situates the lifted library and shared urban ground in Xidan’s wider street network.

05 / Tectonics & models

From boundary to built form.

Envelope studies explore changing degrees of transparency and reflection; physical models test how the lifted volume, central void and illuminated civic interior read together.

A / Envelope

Transparent, translucent, reflective.

Facade and assembly drawings explore possible screen conditions in relation to the structure and library interior. Their operational performance remains to be verified.

01 / Surface Three facade states test the visual relationship between a screened exterior and the activity inside.

02 / Interior The wall section studies inhabited thresholds, bookshelves and their supporting assembly.

03 / Exterior A roof-and-screen detail considers the connection between a planted roof, enclosure and structure.

B / Physical study

Light, volume and void.

Three photographs study the model’s civic presence at night, its illuminated public ground and the relationship between the removable shell and the interior.

Scope & development

This academic design proposal studies public circulation, programme, the urban ground, spatial section, envelope and structural expression. Detailed engineering, structural deflection, facade mechanisms, acoustic separation, fire strategy, access and maintenance would need further validation before construction.
