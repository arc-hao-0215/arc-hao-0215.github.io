03 / Portrait rights & privacy

People within the image

Photographs on this website may include people in streets, public spaces, research encounters, events and archival records. Their presence is not an indication that they have endorsed the research or this website.

The copyright status of an image is distinct from the portrait, privacy, publicity and other personality rights of individuals depicted in it. A Creative Commons licence, a public-domain notice, or photography in a public place does not by itself establish consent for every use of an identifiable person's image.

Consideration and context

Images are selected to support architectural and urban research rather than to identify, profile or make assumptions about individuals. Photographs that prominently depict identifiable people call for particular care. This includes children, direct participants in fieldwork and people shown in potentially sensitive circumstances, such as emergencies, displacement or disability-related settings.

Where an image raises reasonable concerns, its relevance, context, permissions and potential impact should be reviewed. Depending on the circumstances, an image may be recropped, appropriately obscured, substituted or removed. Public visibility does not remove the need for this consideration.

If you appear in a photograph

If you recognise yourself or someone you represent in an image published on this website and have concerns about its display or caption, you are welcome to request a review through the contact information below. Requests can concern identification, context, privacy or removal, even where the photograph itself has a stated copyright licence.

No blanket claim is made that every person depicted has given consent. Each situation depends on the specific image, its context, applicable law, and any relevant permissions or legal exceptions.

04 / Rights & privacy enquiries

Corrections and removal requests

If you are a copyright holder, a person depicted in an image, or an authorised representative, and believe a photograph or other material has been used or credited inappropriately, please contact haochang0215@gmail.com. Concerns about portrait rights, privacy, attribution, context and permission are welcome.

Requests will be reviewed promptly. Where appropriate, the image or caption may be corrected, obscured, replaced or removed while the concern is assessed.

Please include the webpage link, a brief description of the image, and the nature of your concern. You do not need to include sensitive personal details in your first message; any additional information reasonably needed to evaluate the request can be discussed privately.
