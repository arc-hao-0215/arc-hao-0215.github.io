Waterfront+

Shichahai, BeijingUrban design & heritage renewal2024

How can the waterfront reconnect with the neighbourhood behind it?

Waterfront+ treats the lake edge, lanes, courtyards and public spaces as one connected urban fabric. Selective passages and differentiated thresholds reconnect everyday neighbourhood life with the water, while courtyard renewal and landscape adjustments retain the grain of the historic district.

Gold Award · Urban Design · China Human Settlements Academic Year Award, 2025

01

Reconnect the urban seam.

The proposal works through the interface of waterfront, street and courtyard, opening selected connections while retaining differentiated residential thresholds.

02

Work within the inherited grain.

Courtyard and sectional studies test how public passage, domestic space and the water’s edge can coexist. The project remains a design proposition, rather than a record of completed renewal.
