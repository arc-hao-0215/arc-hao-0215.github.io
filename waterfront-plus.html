Waterfront+

Shichahai Waterfront & Heritage Renewal

Shichahai, BeijingUrban Design · Public Realm · Heritage Renewal2024

Gold Award · Urban Design · China Human Settlements Academic Year Award, 2025

How can a historic waterfront become part of everyday neighbourhood life?

PROJECT PROPOSITION

Shichahai is shaped by the interdependence of water, hutong courtyards, cultural landmarks and everyday commerce. Yet proximity to the lakes does not necessarily translate into public access or meaningful neighbourhood connections.

Waterfront+ proposes a sequence of selective openings, adapted courtyards and shared civic spaces. Rather than replacing the historic fabric with a continuous waterfront development, the project seeks to reconnect water, heritage and daily life through targeted interventions.

IN THIS PROJECTContextSite issueApproachMasterplanCommercialCultureCommunityDrawings
01 / URBAN CONTEXT

A layered waterfront within the historic city.

Shichahai is shaped by waterways, temple precincts, commercial streets and fine-grained residential neighbourhoods. The historical study is presented as one coherent original graphic composition.

02 / SITE ISSUE

The lake is close. The connections are fragmented.

Waterfront proximity alone does not guarantee accessible public space. Broken pedestrian links, uneven courtyard redevelopment and disjointed public landscapes weaken connections with the surrounding community.

The challenge is not simply to create more waterfront space, but to restore relationships between places that already coexist.
03 / DESIGN APPROACH

From an urban edge to a civic landscape.

The proposal connects water, cultural landmarks, commercial gateways and community courtyards through carefully differentiated spatial interventions.

Project analysis & design framework +
04 / URBAN MASTERPLAN

One waterfront, multiple scales of civic life.

The overall plan shows how selected projects connect across the historic fabric, rather than imposing a single continuous new object along the lake.

URBAN SYSTEM

A network of places, not a singular waterfront object.

At the district scale, Waterfront+ links commercial entrances, cultural landmarks, waterfront promenades and residential courtyards while preserving the diversity of their spatial characters.

05 / SPATIAL INTERVENTION

Commercial

Urban gateway as public ground.

At Di’anmen, commercial space, transport infrastructure and the waterfront are considered together. A renewed gateway improves pedestrian connections and creates shared urban ground.

06 / SPATIAL INTERVENTION

Culture

Keeping heritage within everyday public life.

Around Huoshen Temple, public activities, water and landscape form an inhabited setting that supports cultural memory without isolating historic architecture from local life.

07 / SPATIAL INTERVENTION

Community

Courtyards as units of gradual renewal.

In Baimi Xiejie, a sequence of adapted courtyard buildings, a neighbourhood library and shared spaces supports local everyday life while respecting residential privacy.

08 / ARCHITECTURAL DRAWINGS

Read the waterfront in plan and section.

Each district is presented as one coherent plan-and-section sheet, retaining the original adjacency and vertical relationship between drawings.

SCOPE & DEVELOPMENT

Waterfront+ is an urban-design proposal. Ownership, resident agreements, conservation review and the management of shared facilities require further study before implementation. The related research separately explores the social and spatial consequences of waterfront renewal.
