Ziqiang Technology Building
Atrium Design

Beijing, ChinaCompetition · 1st Prize2024

How can an atrium become a shared learning landscape?

The atrium proposal turns underused circulation into places for informal learning, conversation and gathering. Flexible occupation and a connected interior landscape extend the building’s life beyond the classroom. The design received first prize in the competition.

1st Prize · Atrium Design Competition

01

Circulation becomes social space.

Flexible seating, shared surfaces and planted space turn the atrium into an everyday setting for informal learning. The plan and model describe the first-prize competition proposal.
