Learning Beyond

Beijing, ChinaCompetition & commission2024–

What if circulation space became a place to stay?

A proposal for Tsinghua’s teaching spaces transforms underused atriums into flexible settings for social learning. The 2024 competition work is presented here alongside a subsequent commission: co-leading the First Teaching Building renovation commission from May 2025.

Teaching Building Public Space Competition — 1st Prize

01

Circulation becomes social space.

The 2024 competition proposal reworks more than 800 square metres of underused atriums. These drawings document that proposal; they are not presented as completed construction or as final documentation of the later commission.

From proposal
to practice.

A subsequent 1,500 m² renovation commission for Tsinghua’s First Teaching Building, co-led from May 2025. The work involves preservation constraints, coordination with university administration, and integrating contemporary building services into the historic fabric.

Project credits

Hao’s role: concept, modeling, renderings
Instructor: Martijn de Geus
