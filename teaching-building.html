Learning in Between

Green Heart — Ziqiang Technology Building Atrium

Tsinghua University, BeijingCampus Atrium · Informal Learning · Adaptive Reuse2024

1st Prize · Ziqiang Technology Building Atrium Design Competition

Project proposition

How can learning extend beyond the classroom?

University life increasingly unfolds beyond scheduled lectures and enclosed classrooms. Yet conventional teaching buildings often prioritise movement and efficiency over opportunities to pause, exchange ideas, or learn informally.

Learning in Between reimagines the atrium of the Ziqiang Technology Building as a shared learning landscape. Stepped gathering spaces, integrated planting, study pods, and flexible furniture transform an overlooked interior into a setting for collective and individual learning.

01 / Context

The spaces between classrooms deserve a life of their own.

Traditional teaching buildings separate classrooms, offices and circulation into clearly defined zones. While supporting scheduled activities, this organisation offers limited opportunities for spontaneous interaction and informal exchange. The existing atrium, with access to daylight and nearby greenery, offers a starting point for reconnecting learning, movement and nature.

02 / Design approach

A seed for a more open campus.

Green Heart introduces a shared green landscape into the centre of the existing building. Rather than treating the atrium as an isolated interior, the proposal imagines a prototype for campus public space—one whose openness, greenery and opportunities for exchange might extend outwards over time.

03 / Spatial organisation

Circulation becomes a place to stay.

The atrium is reorganised as a connected sequence of learning environments across multiple levels. Stepped gathering spaces support collective activities, while smaller study settings and planted edges offer different degrees of exposure and privacy without losing visual connections across the shared void.

03 / Third floor

Third Floor — Collective Forum

The third-floor plan converts circulation into a collective forum. Tiered seating, flexible tables and open gathering areas create places where scheduled activities and informal encounters can overlap.

Rather than acting only as passage, the atrium becomes a shared destination at the centre of the floor.

04 / Architectural elements

A kit of spaces for different ways of learning.

Rather than prescribing one use, lightweight components are introduced within the existing structure. Timber finishes, modular furnishings and integrated planting form a coherent architectural language while responding to the practical limits of adapting a university building.

An adaptable framework / Seven elements

From small retreats to shared gatherings.

Seven complementary insertions extend learning beyond scheduled classrooms, from solitary work to public events. Together they form a connected, adaptable interior.

  • 01
    Atrium planting

    Planted edges and green railings soften the atrium, bringing daylight, vegetation and everyday circulation into closer contact.

  • 02
    Group study rooms

    Semi-open collaboration spaces accommodate team projects, peer learning and informal discussion without isolating users from the central void.

  • 03
    Individual study rooms

    Smaller enclosed retreats offer quiet places for reading or focused work, balancing concentration with a visual connection to shared life.

  • 04
    Multifunctional bookshelves

    Modular shelving combines reading, display and spatial division, shaping study niches while keeping the learning resources visible and accessible.

  • 05
    Multifunctional steps

    Terraced platforms connect levels and double as informal seating, small-group meeting points and collective viewing areas.

  • 06
    Meeting rooms

    More defined spaces accommodate tutorials and group meetings, supporting focused exchange alongside the open communal areas.

  • 07
    Presentation forum

    An open central platform provides a flexible stage for student presentations, public conversations and temporary campus events.

Spatial adaptability / 02 modes

One space, multiple possibilities.

The same spatial framework shifts between assembly and display through movable furniture, while the surrounding learning spaces stay active.

01 / LECTURE MODE

Lecture Mode

Stepped seating becomes an informal auditorium for talks and discussion, with quieter study settings at the edges.

02 / EXHIBITION MODE

Exhibition Mode

Movable displays create a setting for student exhibitions while circulation flows around the planted centre.

05 / Spatial experience

Between collective life and individual retreat.

Green Heart moves gradually from the openness of the central forum to quieter places at its edges. Natural light, planting and layered views give individual study and social activity room to coexist as part of everyday campus life.

Scope & development

Green Heart is a first-prize competition proposal for the Ziqiang Technology Building atrium. It studies a light intervention within an existing university building; structural coordination, accessibility, acoustics, planting maintenance and detailed specifications would be resolved during further design development. This proposal is distinct from the later commissioned First Teaching Building renewal.
