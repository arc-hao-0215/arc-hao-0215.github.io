Their Story

Hong KongCommunity research2024

How can everyday stories become a shared cultural record?

A community calendar project in Hong Kong’s Eastern District, developed through field research sponsored by Swire Properties. The work brings local stories into a printed format, extending architectural attention toward the people and cultures that give a neighbourhood its character.

Context

Developed during the Swire Properties Bi-city Youth Cultural Leadership Programme, this work sits alongside comparative study of urban placemaking and cultural ecosystems in Beijing and Hong Kong.
