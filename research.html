Research / An ongoing enquiry

Reading places.
Tracing relationships.

How does architecture intervene when existing territorial relationships become fragmented, vulnerable or historically layered?

Eleven essays investigate how architecture becomes a public institution, a working landscape or a form of care. Field observations, documentary evidence and comparative readings develop arguments that are tested separately in the design projects.
