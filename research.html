01 / Water & collective life

Adaptation as an everyday practice.

In Jakarta, flood response is inseparable from access to clinics, markets, work, and shared space. The architectural question is how those relationships can endure through changing conditions.

Intervention begins with what is there.

The sacred route of Kailash and the military terrain of Penghu ask different questions of restraint. Both make landscape, existing structures, and cultural sequence active participants in design.
