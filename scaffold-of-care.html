Scaffold of Care

An Infrastructure of Coexistence

Zhongli, TaoyuanMixed-Use Civic ArchitectureMigration · Public Space · Care2025

Longlisted · ArchDaily Student Project Awards 2025 ↗

Project proposition

A place to stay, not only to pass through.

In Zhongli, a city shaped by migration, the streets surrounding the railway station provide essential places for encounter—yet migrant communities often lack welcoming spaces in which to gather without having to consume or move along.

Scaffold of Care transforms an overlooked gap on Changjiang Road into a shared civic living room. A lightweight and adaptable construction system becomes a framework for cultural life, everyday services and collective belonging.

01 / Context

The living room deficit.

Near Zhongli Station, commercial streets, gathering points and the river lie close together but remain socially and spatially fragmented. Existing patterns of public life reveal the need for places of rest, recognition and exchange.

01 / Everyday occupation

Gathering already happens.

On days off, familiar streets and shopfronts become informal social rooms for meeting, eating and spending time together.

02 / Spatial exclusion

Visibility is not belonging.

Communities sustain local life while often having limited access to places where their presence is welcomed without condition.

03 / Urban possibility

A gap can become common ground.

The Changjiang Road site offers an opportunity to reconnect pedestrian life, neighbourhood activity and the river corridor.

02 / Urban strategy

Reclaiming an urban gap.

The proposal establishes a civic address without enclosing the site as a single-purpose institution. A porous scaffold gives collective activities a legible place among the surrounding streets and buildings.

Urban proposition

Temporary structure, lasting public claim.

The lightweight frame stands apart from the permanent buildings around it. Rather than serving one group or programme, it offers a shared threshold for movement, encounters and informal occupation.

Small in footprint but generous in use, the intervention connects street-level activity with gathering places at several levels.

03 / Everyday life

Coexistence through ordinary rituals.

Care takes shape through everyday actions: sharing food, learning, sitting together and celebrating distinct traditions. The scaffold makes room for different communities to be present on their own terms.

04 / Assembly

A kit of shared spaces.

Reconfigurable scaffold bays and lightweight additions form a flexible civic framework. Construction is imagined not as a fixed object, but as a system responsive to changing community needs.

Programmes of care

One framework, many ways to belong.

The spatial kit accommodates different rhythms of social life without assigning a single identity to the building.

ShareCross-cultural kitchens, communal tables and neighbourhood events.
LearnWorkshops, co-learning and informal community classrooms.
SupportPotential NGO-led advice, legal consultation and other community services.
From space to place
Coexistence is not about integration, but about the dignity of co-presence.

The proposal begins with cultural life that already exists in Zhongli. Its architectural ambition is to make room for that life to continue, grow and be recognised. The building remains unbuilt; structure, accessibility, fire safety and long-term stewardship require further development.
