INTRODUCTION / ACROSS MEDIA

Other ways of observing, making, and inhabiting.

Architecture is only one way of exploring the relationship between people, space, and narrative. Drawing, performance, and editorial design offer different tools for observation, imagination, and collective expression.

These selected studies move between the visual page and the inhabited stage, exploring how stories are composed, how bodies occupy space, and how images make ideas tangible.

03A / COMMUNITY EDITORIAL

Their Story

Community calendar & editorial design · Eastern District, Hong Kong · 2024

Developed through community research in Hong Kong’s Eastern District, Their Story translates local narratives into a printed calendar. Visual sequence, typography and editing give those stories a recurring place in everyday life.

Developed through the Swire Properties Bi-city Youth Cultural Leadership Programme; field research sponsored by Swire Properties.
