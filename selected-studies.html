Selected Other Work

Across mediaArt & performance2017–2024

Other ways of observing, making, and inhabiting.

Design approach

Drawing, photography, graphic design and performance form a parallel practice of making and observation. The works below are organised through their media and roles: images composed on a page, space recorded through a lens, and movement developed with performers. Together they show other ways of working with attention, sequence, material and collective occupation.

Scope & development

Individual works and collaborative roles are identified in their captions. This collection remains the final entry in the chronological portfolio.
