Other Ways of Making

Art, Performance & Editorial Design

Across MediaDrawing · Editorial Design · Photography · Performance2017–2024

Other ways of observing, making, and inhabiting.

Design approach

Drawing, editorial design, photography and performance form a parallel practice of making and observation. The works below are organised through their media and roles: images composed on a page, space recorded through a lens, and movement developed with performers. Together they show other ways of working with attention, sequence, material and collective occupation.

Their Story

Community Calendar & Editorial Design · Eastern District, Hong Kong · 2024

Developed through community research in Hong Kong's Eastern District, Their Story translates local narratives into a printed calendar. Editing, visual sequencing, typography and the relationship between image and text place community experiences within an object encountered repeatedly throughout the year.

The work was developed through the Swire Properties Bi-city Youth Cultural Leadership Programme; field research was sponsored by Swire Properties. It belongs within this collection of graphic and cultural work, rather than standing as a separate architecture project.
