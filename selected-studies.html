Art, Performance & Editorial Design

Other ways of observing, making, and inhabiting.

Across media

Architecture is only one of the ways I explore the relationship between people, space, and narrative. Through drawing, performance, and editorial design, these works investigate how stories are constructed, how bodies occupy space, and how ideas take visual form.

03A / COMMUNITY EDITORIAL

Their Story

Community calendar & editorial design · Eastern District, Hong Kong · 2024

Developed through community research in Hong Kong’s Eastern District, Their Story translates local narratives into a printed calendar. Through visual sequencing, typography and editing, the project brings community experiences into an object encountered throughout the year.

Developed through the Swire Properties Bi-city Youth Cultural Leadership Programme; field research sponsored by Swire Properties.
